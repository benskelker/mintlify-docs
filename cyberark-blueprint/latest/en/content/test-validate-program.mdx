This topic describes the importance of testing and validating identity security programs after implementation, to check the effectiveness of the work that’s been conducted. These processes not only ensure that security measures are actively protecting organizational assets, but they also help maintain compliance with evolving regulations. By thoroughly testing and validating their security program, organizations can ensure that they’re actually accomplishing the goals they set out to complete.

Review the program
--------------------------------

Start by conducting a review of your program. This step involves revisiting the identity security commitments that the organization has stated they completed and verifying that they have been implemented as planned. For instance, running reports from the Identity Security Platform can validate that practices such as password rotation are being enforced and that there is adherence to MFA assurance levels.

Test the defenses
-------------------------------

It’s also recommended to periodically test your identity security measures to assess their effectiveness. This can be achieved through internal testing and more aggressive external red-team exercises. Such testing simulates potential attack scenarios to identify vulnerabilities before they can be exploited by malicious actors. This is about challenging the system to ensure that the security infrastructure is robust and can withstand real-world attacks.

Validate against real world scenarios
---------------------------------------------------

This topic explains the importance of testing and validating identity security programs post-implementation, covering steps such as reviewing the program, testing defenses, validating against real-world scenarios, and remediating identified issues.

Whenever there are reports of major global cybersecurity breaches, organizations should analyze them to determine if the vulnerabilities exploited could affect their systems. This proactive approach enables an organization to assess whether their current controls would be effective against similar threats and make necessary adjustments.

Remediate issues
------------------------------

The outcome of these reviews and tests must be documented and analyzed. Identifying what measures succeeded, which failed, and where gaps lie is crucial for continuous improvement. Developing a detailed remediation plan to address these gaps will help strengthen the security framework, reduce potential risks, and enhance your overall resilience against identity threats.

Together, these steps create a proactive, responsive identity security program that not only meets the set security standards but evolves with the changing landscape of cyber threats, ensuring better protection and compliance.
