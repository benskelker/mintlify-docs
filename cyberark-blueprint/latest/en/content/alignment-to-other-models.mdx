This topic describes the alignment of the CyberArk Identity attack chain with other industry attack frameworks.

The CyberArk Identity attack chain is centered around the identity-related risks and tactics, techniques, and procedures that occur in the overarching attack chain. You’ll find similarities between our guiding principles and other industry attack frameworks, such as [Lockheed Martins’ Cyber Kill Chain®](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/capabilities/cyber/cyber-kill-chain.html), [MITRE’s ATT&CK™](https://attack.mitre.org/resources/getting-started/) and [The Unified Kill Chain](https://www.unifiedkillchain.com/). These attack frameworks typically focus on a wider scope of cybersecurity exploitations and controls, of which identity is one aspect, whereas the CyberArk Blueprint is focused on identity-centric attacks.

These frameworks often outline more detailed tactics, techniques, and procedures that malicious actors will use to establish entry, steal credentials, move laterally and vertically, and escalate and abuse privileges.

If you subscribe to one of these cyber threat intelligence frameworks, we encourage you to also leverage the CyberArk Blueprint to help identify and align the key areas where identity security controls mitigate risk in their various models.
