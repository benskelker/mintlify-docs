Cybersecurity leaders are often challenged with reporting on the success of their security initiatives, balancing the need to present complex information in a concise and compelling manner without losing impact or meaning. Even more challenging is ensuring that this is done well up and down the security leadership chain and that everyone from the Program Manager up to the CISO and the Board of Directors is speaking a common language. To aid in this storytelling effort, we have developed success measurement and program reporting guidance.
