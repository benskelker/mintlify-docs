Identity Administration typically complete a release every month. You can view release notes for current and previous releases using the following links.

*   [Identity Administration Release Notes - Latest](/identity-administration/latest/en/content/releasenotes/releasenotes-latest)

*   [Identity Administration Release Notes - Previous](/identity-administration/latest/en/content/releasenotes/release-notes-previous-versions)

You are not required to take any action in preparation for or after a release.

Current release rollout process
---------------------------------------------

Identity Administration progressively rolls out releases in stages to groups of customers, known as PODS. PODS are created based on region, time zones, and other factors.

CyberArk may update its release process from time to time. The information in this section does not constitute a commitment or an obligation to deliver any feature or functionality. The development, release, and timing of any future feature or functionality in our products remains at CyberArk’s sole discretion.

Subscribe to release notifications
------------------------------------------------

CyberArk publishes release timelines on the [CyberArk Identity Cloud Status](https://trust.idaptive.com/) page. On the status page, you can subscribe to receive email updates for your POD whenever CyberArk publishes, creates, updates, or resolves an event. After subscribing to notifications for your POD, you will receive release notifications at least 48 hours before a release.

To find your pod, click your username, then click About. Note the pod in the About window.
