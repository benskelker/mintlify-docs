Invite users to the CyberArk platform
---------------------------------------------------

To invite users

1.   Go to Core Services &gt; Users, then click Invite Users.

2.   Search for the user, group, or role that you want to invite, then select the object and click Invite.

You can filter your search by Directory source and User status.

CyberArk sends an email to the selected users with a link to sign in to the service.

If you encounter a problem with email notifications, contact [CyberArk Support](https://www.cyberark.com/services-support/technical-support-contact/).
