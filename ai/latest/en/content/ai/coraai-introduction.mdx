By integrating AI into the CyberArk Identity Security Platform, CyberArk offers a friendlier and easier onboarding and work experience. AI-based tools help your administrators navigate the setup and ongoing maintenance of your system. AI is also integrated into the main functionality of the system, to give you valuable insights into your operational settings and to offer helpful recommendations for your setup or your next steps. AI-based assistance helps you deploy CyberArk solutions and improve system efficiency.

CORA AI capabilities
----------------------------------

CyberArk's CORA AI is integrated into various ISPSS services and engines to facilitate your tasks and improve your work experience.

CyberArk's CORA AI capabilities| AI capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| CyberArk CORA AI chatbot | Enables you to engage with CyberArk's services through natural language to post queries, gain important information about the system, run commands, and easily set up and maintain your system. This helps you to quickly gain confidence in performing your setup and administrative tasks and subsequently update and maintain your system. CORA AI initially supports actions in CyberArk Privilege Cloud and Audit. Additional services will soon be available. For more information, see [CyberArk's CORA AI chatbot](/ai/latest/en/content/ai/coraai-chatbot). |
| CORA AI - Functional insights and recommendations | CyberArk CORA AI capabilities are based on operational experience and know-how that CyberArk has amassed over many years in managing identity security systems. CORA AI is integrated into the key functions for securing user identities and access. This includes account discovery and recommendations for offboarding accounts. These features help you quickly find important accounts or remove those that are no longer needed. As CyberArk's CORA AI engines evolve, you can rely on their insights and support to make your tasks easier to accomplish. |
