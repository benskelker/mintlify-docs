This section describes how to integrate third-party SIEM applications with Audit information such as activities and events.

To use the Export to SIEM feature in Administration, your assigned role must have the Show Export to SIEM in Administration space administrative right.

The Show Export to SIEM in Administration space administrative right is assigned to the following roles by default:

*   Administration Role

*   global auditor

The SIEM integration API retrieves events from the previous 7 days only.
