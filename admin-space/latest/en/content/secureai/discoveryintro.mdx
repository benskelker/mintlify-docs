Use discovery scanners to identify AWS Bedrock, AWS AgentCore, and Copilot Studio AI agents across your environment. Deploy the scanners and view discovered agents in the Discovery & Context unified dashboard, which provides risk insights and recommended remediation steps.

Discovered AI agents are viewed in the Discovery & Context dashboard. Secure AI agents is used to register and secure AI agents, but it does not display the discovered agents themselves.

For details about Discovery & Context, see Analyze the AI agent inventory and remediate risks.
