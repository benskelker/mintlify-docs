This section explains how to design and manage access control policies that enforce zero standing privileges (ZSP) across your cloud and infrastructure environments.

Access policies are used to define who can access specific assets—such as developers, IT administrators, or third-party contractors—and when they can access them.

By provisioning access on a just-in-time (JIT)basis and for a limited duration, these policies help reduce the attack surface, mitigate the risk of credential misuse, and support compliance with least privilege principles.

You can define access policies for the following:

*   Cloud workspaces: Provides users with JIT access to cloud consoles

*   Databases: Provides users with JIT access to databases

*   Virtual machines: Provides users with JIT access to organizational assets
