This topic explains CyberArk's usage of AI and how to enable or disable AI features in your tenant.

Overview
----------------------

CyberArk integrates various AI tools into its products to enhance security, streamline processes, and provide advanced predictive capabilities. These AI features, powered by both in-house and third-party services, are designed to deliver valuable insights while maintaining high standards of privacy and security. While AI features are enabled by default, you always have the option to disable these features if desired.

For information about our responsible AI policy, see [CyberArk Responsible AI Policy](https://www.cyberark.com/trust/responsible-ai/).

You can disable or enable AI features from the AI preferences page. This action currently controls the following AI features:

*   CyberArk[CORA AI Chatbot](/ai/latest/en/content/ai/coraai-introduction) deployed on Privilege Cloud, Audit, and Identity services.

*   Anomalous Retrieval of Secrets (ARS) detection in ISI.

*   [CORA AI textual summaries of recorded SSH sessions performed through Secure Infrastructure Access (SIA)](/audit/latest/en/content/audit/isp_session-activities)

*   [CORA AI textual audits of recorded RDP sessions performed through Secure Infrastructure Access (SIA)](/audit/latest/en/content/audit/isp_session-activities)

*   Onboard discovered accounts by using [AI-based recommendations from the Remediation agent](https://docs.cyberark.com/early-release/remediation-agent/content/discovery/discovery-ai-recommendations.htm)
