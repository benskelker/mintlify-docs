This topic describes where to find license consumption information and to understand the CyberArk service entitlement lifecycle.

Review license consumption information
----------------------------------------------------

You can review license consumption information in the License consumption page.

To navigate to the License consumption page, in the Administration space, go to My environment &gt;Environment management &gt;License consumption.

License consumption includes the following details about your license usage per service:

*   Time that the displayed license usage information was last updated

*   (Optional per service)Total number of licenses in use out of total number of available licenses

*   Number of licenses in use out of number of available licenses per license type (the types vary per service)

This feature is currently available for Privilege Cloud (see more information in the Privilege Cloud docs) and CyberArk Identity (see more information in the [CyberArk Identity docs](https://docs.cyberark.com/privilege-cloud-shared-services/latest/en/content/coreservices/getstarted/ispss-user-roles.htm "link to topic in CyberArk Identity docs that explains licensing")).

If you want to see license usage information for another ISPSS service, contact your CyberArk representative.

[](https://docs.cyberark.com/admin-space/latest/en/content/environment/as-license-consumption.htm)ISPSS service lifecycle
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CyberArk manages the service lifecycle in the ISPSS environment according to the entitlement period and its renewal.

The following table presents the different stages of the ISPSS service lifecycle.

ISPSS entitlement lifecycle| Service status | Description and recommended actions |
| --- | --- |
| Active | * All CyberArk services in your ISPSS environment require valid and active entitlements, which are granted based on your purchased licenses * During the entitlement period, the service is available for use |
| Expiration | * Some time before the entitlement period ends, CyberArk personnel will contact you to discuss service renewal * If the entitlement is not renewed, it will expire at the end of the initial entitlement period |
| Suspension | * After the entitlement period expires, the related service is suspended * Access is restricted and the service is no longer available * Service availability depends on your renewal decision: * If the entitlement is renewed, the service is reactivated * If the entitlement is not renewed, the service is permanently discontinued |
| Deletion | CyberArk does not delete the service immediately after suspension. It retains the service, along with its associated data and configurations, for a minimum of seven (7) days before permanent removal from the tenant. |
