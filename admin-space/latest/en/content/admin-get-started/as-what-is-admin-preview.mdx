CyberArk Identity Security Platform Shared Services provides a comprehensive suite of services for identity security and access management, as well as shared services such as audits, connector management, and user management. While this approach offers a single platform for accessing CyberArk business services and various shared services, we want to take it a step further and introduce an even more unified user experience.

Our goal with the Administration space, is to create a unified platform that streamlines administrative processes. By consolidating related tasks into cohesive areas and aligning them with common user scenarios, we aim to reduce the need for interface switches and simplify navigation. As part of this initiative, we are gradually transitioning from services to spaces in our UI.

At this stage, some flows and services are already unified, and more will be integrated in the near future. As we continue to develop and integrate these improvements, we invite you to explore and provide feedback on the new platform to help us refine and enhance the overall user experience.
