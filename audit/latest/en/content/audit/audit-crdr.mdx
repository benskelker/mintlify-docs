This topic describes CyberArk's Cross-Region Disaster Recovery (CRDR) capability.

CRDR overview
---------------------------

CyberArk’s Cross-Region Disaster Recovery (CRDR) ensures service continuity if an AWS region becomes unavailable by providing a fully managed standby environment in another region.

It extends resilience beyond in-region high availability to protect against complete regional outages. During a failure, users can continue authenticating normally, access required credentials through the Secure Access Space, and retain full Audit visibility with minimal disruption.

For more information about how a CRDR incident impacts Audit functionality, see [CRDR Audit functionality](/cross-region-dr/latest/en/content/crdr/crdr-audit).

For more information about CRDR Audit codes, see [Cross-Region Disaster Recovery (CRDR) audit events](/audit/latest/en/content/audit/product-audits/isp-crdr-audit).

For more information about CRDR, see [What is Cross-Region Disaster Recovery (CRDR)?](/cross-region-dr/latest/en/content/crdr/crdr-what-is).
