CyberArk’s CyberArk Identity Security Platform Shared Services Shared Services unify administrative processes across CyberArk SaaS solutions and drives operational efficiencies for security teams.

Advantages of CyberArk Identity Security Platform Shared Services - the Shared Services platform
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Shared Services platform offers the advantages of unified and efficient workflows and a single user experience to users of CyberArk's SaaS products:

*   Simplified user administration thanks to centralized role-based permissions, no need for LDAP certificate management, and support of cloud directories

*   Efficient security management thanks to centralized SSO, MFA, and lifecycle management

*   Efficient access to system functionality thanks to SaaS services replacing on-premises services such as Discovery.

*   AI-led user experience thanks to CORA AI that provides an easier onboarding and work experience for setup, administration, and maintenance activities.
