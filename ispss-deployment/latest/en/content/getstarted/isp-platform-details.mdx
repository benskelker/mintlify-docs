This topic describes the user information area, where you can view important notifications, manage your profile, refresh your permissions, and view relevant details about your tenant and the ISPSS.

View product notifications
----------------------------------------

In-product notifications are a great way to stay informed about important updates and information relevant to your role. Here's how you can manage them:

*   Security issues: You'll receive toast messages for critical security updates or vulnerabilities. These messages are designed to catch your attention and must be dismissed manually to ensure you've acknowledged them.

*   Scheduled maintenance: Alerts about upcoming maintenance windows and potential downtime will be sent to you. This helps you plan accordingly and avoid unexpected disruptions.

*   New product features: Stay updated on new functionalities through notifications delivered directly to your inbox. You can review these updates at any time from your dashboard by accessing the message list.

Remember, the bell notification icon next to your user profile is your gateway to these important alerts and updates. Make sure to check it regularly to stay on top of things that matter in your role.

Manage your user profile
--------------------------------------

You can manage your security information and personal profile details in the User Portal. To do this, open your user profile in the top right corner of the page and click Manage your account.

For more information about the options available in the Account page, see the [Identity Administration docs](https://docs.cyberark.com/identity/latest/en/content/userportal/update-profile.htm "Link to the Identity Administration docs for managing your authentication details").

View sign-in information
--------------------------------------

You can see the date and time that you last signed in before your current session.

Refresh your user permissions
-------------------------------------------

You can refresh your user permissions in the ISPSS.

1.   Click Reload rights.

2.   In the confirmation message, click Reload rights to refresh your permissions.

View tenant details
---------------------------------

When you click About, the following information is shown:

Tenant details| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Tenant details |
| ID | Your organization's tenant ID |
| Region | The AWS region where the tenant is located. |
| Your service |
| Version | Service version that is installed. |
| Identity Administration |
| ID | The Identity Administration tenant ID |
| Pod ID | The ID of the AWS pod where the Identity Administration tenant is located |
| Region | The AWS region where the Identity Administration tenant is located |

This information may be requested by CyberArk Support if you contact them for assistance.

View ISPSS information
------------------------------------

Sign out of the ISPSS
-----------------------------------

Click Sign out to end your ISPSS session.
