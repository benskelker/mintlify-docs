This topic explains how you can scan your environment to discover entities such as entitled cloud identities and privileged accounts, along with their dependencies in your organization. You can do this in the Identity Protection space, under Discovery & remediations or by running dedicated REST APIs.

Once the scans have been run and identities discovered, you can view and analyze them, and remediate gaps.

What kinds of entities can you discover?
-----------------------------------------------------

You can discover the following types of entities:

*   Accounts: Database accounts, Windows domain and local accounts, Unix local accounts, and MacOS accounts from within the Administrators/root groups.

*   Entitled cloud entities: You can discover human identities (user or groups) that have direct standing cloud access in your cloud environment.

Once the entities have been discovered, you can decide which ones should be securely managed by CyberArk and add them to the system (onboarding). You can also analyze them and remediate any risks that have surfaced.

For information about discovering accounts from integrated platforms, see the [CyberArk Marketplace](https://community.cyberark.com/marketplace/s/#---Privilege_Cloud_Support__c-Privilege%20Cloud%20only~CyberArk_Solution__c-DiscoveryTools).
