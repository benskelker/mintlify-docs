Accelerate your security with the CyberArk Blueprint
------------------------------------------------------------------

The CyberArk Blueprint is a proven framework for identity security success, designed to help organizations establish and evolve an effective identity security program and achieve more impactful security outcomes, faster. The CyberArk Blueprint provides risk-based prescriptive guidance built on the combined knowledge and experience of CyberArk battling threats in the identity security space.

These insights are gathered from more than two decades of lessons learned across CyberArk’s global customer base, post-breach [incident response](https://www.cyberark.com/services-support/remediation/), frontline remediation, [red-team](https://www.cyberark.com/services-support/red-team-services/) engagements and [CyberArk Lab’s](https://labs.cyberark.com/) innovative researchers. The Blueprint is inclusive of security best practices for the full spectrum of identities, including IT, Developers, Workforce and Machine identities.

The Blueprint guides organizations through their identity security journey by helping them to understand the identity attack chain, assess their security posture, build their roadmap, learn best practices, and develop a broader identity security program.

The Blueprint is not just a singular resource; it consists of a broad ecosystem of self-service educational resources and collateral, including videos, documentation, toolkits, and more. Each component provides best-practice guidance across the people, process, and technology domains — all designed to help you achieve more impactful identity security outcomes, faster.

Benefits of using the CyberArk Blueprint
------------------------------------------------------

You can use the Blueprint to:

*   Understand the attack chain - Leverage the Blueprint to better understand the identity attack chain. How do malicious actors exploit your workforce, IT, developer, and machine identities to execute their endgame? And how can you prevent those types of attacks? As good security practitioners, you should strive to put identity security controls in place, which mitigate the risks associated with that attack chain.

*   Assess your security posture - Security assessments are vital to information security. Leverage the Blueprint to accelerate your security efforts, identify weaknesses and security control gaps, and gain insight into your security posture through the lens of prioritization, security control families, and technology groups.
