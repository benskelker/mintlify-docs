This topic explains how you can leverage the CyberArk Blueprint to assess your security posture, either in a self-service or a consultative approach.

Importance of security assessments
------------------------------------------------

Security assessments are a vital part of any information security program. They help you identify areas of weakness and security control gaps and enable you to prioritize your efforts in closing those gaps. With identity being at the center of many breaches and compromises, it’s critical to understand your security posture as it relates to identity security. Organizations that don’t conduct security assessments on an ongoing basis are more likely to be unaware of any gaps and weaknesses that exist.

Security assessments can be done as an internal exercise only, run by members of your organization, or with the help of a third party leveraging a consultative approach. In either case, it’s important that you consider ongoing security assessments to determine your vulnerabilities and risk.

Self-assessment workbook[](https://docs.cyberark.com/identity-protection-space/latest/en/content/blueprint/assess-security-posture.htm)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To help expedite the process of assessing your own security posture and to give organizations a quick and easy way to understand how they stack up against CyberArk’s best practices for identity security, we’ve created the [CyberArk Blueprint Self-Assessment Workbook](https://cyberark.my.site.com/s/article/Assessing-Your-Security-Posture-with-the-Blueprint-Self-Assessment-Workbook).

Self-review the use cases and recommended controls in the CyberArk Blueprint. Indicate if the technologies exist within your organization, and if they do, provide an estimated percentage of the use case covered by the recommended security controls. The workbook can then display visualizations based on your responses, giving you a quick readout on where you stand, and helping you accelerate your security efforts.

[![Image 3](https://docs.cyberark.com/identity-protection-space/latest/en/content/resources/images/blueprint-images/self-assessment-workbook.png)](https://docs.cyberark.com/identity-protection-space/latest/en/content/resources/images/blueprint-images/self-assessment-workbook.png)

This self-assessment workbook is not a replacement for a fully-fledged internal or external security review; it’s not a replacement for a governance risk and compliance tool and it’s only as accurate as your prior knowledge or research. The workbook lets you get a quick readout of how your organization is doing compared to CyberArk’s best practices and should be used in conjunction with other assessment methodologies. Since it’s based on the CyberArk Blueprint, our best practice recommendations for building a successful identity Security program, it makes assumptions about asset types, security threats and vulnerabilities, risks, and priorities.

Consultative approach
-----------------------------------

It’s common for organizations to also leverage a consultative approach to assessing their security posture, often in collaboration with an Advisory Partner, which can significantly enhance the effectiveness and precision of the assessment. Advisory Partners play a critical role in providing a nuanced external perspective during the security assessment process. These partners, equipped with deep industry insights and experience can blend their proprietary knowledge and assessment methodologies with the perspective and guidance from CyberArk to give you a robust and much deeper security assessment. These paid engagements vary in duration, scope, and deliverables.

CyberArk offers consultative assessment services through our [Strategic Consulting](https://www.cyberark.com/services-support/strategic-consulting/) offering, as well as through our [Red Team](https://www.cyberark.com/services-support/red-team-services/) services.
