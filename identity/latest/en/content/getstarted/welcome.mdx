System overview
-----------------------------

CyberArk Identity is composed of the following services, web portals for administrators and users, and mobile applications users can install on their iOS and Android devices.

[](https://docs.cyberark.com/identity/latest/en/content/getstarted/welcome.htm)CyberArk Identity component locations
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The services that are a part of your CyberArk Identity tenant are hosted in different regions based on pods. A tenant is the logical unit that separates subscriber organizations from each other within CyberArk Identity. When you sign in to CyberArk Identity, you sign in to a specific tenant. Every operation that you perform applies to that tenant alone.

To find your pod, click your username, then click About. Note the pod in the About window.

![Image 6](https://docs.cyberark.com/identity/latest/en/content/coreservices/getstarted/images/aboutmenu-pods.png)
</Frame>
